PLUGIN_SHAZAMCAPTURE_SETTINGS_EMPTY

PLUGIN_SHAZAMCAPTURE_SHOW_SPOTIFY_HISTORY
	EN	Show Spotify information in Shazam History

PLUGIN_SHAZAMCAPTURE_SHOW_SPOTIFY_HISTORY_DESC
	EN	Show the Spotify link or missing-link message on history detail pages. Recognition and database storage are unaffected.

PLUGIN_SHAZAMCAPTURE_FLUSH_SAME_STREAM_METADATA
	EN	Clear recognition audio when metadata changes on the same stream

PLUGIN_SHAZAMCAPTURE_FLUSH_SAME_STREAM_METADATA_DESC
	EN	When enabled, pause PCM collection as soon as metadata changes on an unchanged stream. After the change remains stable for two seconds, collection restarts from zero and identification waits for a fully fresh sample. Applies to Radio, SpotOn, and other sources that reuse one stream. Disabled by default.

PLUGIN_SHAZAMCAPTURE_SAVE_DEBUG_WAV
	EN	Save recognition audio for debugging

PLUGIN_SHAZAMCAPTURE_SAVE_DEBUG_WAV_DESC
	EN	Keep the exact normalized WAV sent to Shazam in var/dumps. Files are named from the result title and local date/time, or NoResult when no title is returned. Files must be deleted manually. Disabled by default.

PLUGIN_SHAZAMCAPTURE_AUTO_RECOGNITION
	EN	Automatically recognize radio streams

PLUGIN_SHAZAMCAPTURE_AUTO_RECOGNITION_DESC
	EN	Continuously identify eligible Radio and HLS playlist streams on each player. Other plugin sources such as Spotty are excluded. Disabled by default.

PLUGIN_SHAZAMCAPTURE_AUTO_METADATA_OVERLAY
	EN	Replace radio metadata with automatic recognition results

PLUGIN_SHAZAMCAPTURE_AUTO_METADATA_OVERLAY_DESC
	EN	Publish title, artist, album, and artwork from successful automatic samples. Manual samples never change now-playing metadata. This setting is used only when automatic recognition is enabled.

PLUGIN_SHAZAMCAPTURE_AUTO_IGNORED_STATIONS
	EN	Stations excluded from automatic recognition

PLUGIN_SHAZAMCAPTURE_AUTO_IGNORED_STATIONS_DESC
	EN	Comma-separated LMS station names matched without regard to capitalization, for example: Yacht Radio, Wave FM.

PLUGIN_SHAZAMCAPTURE_AUTO_COOLDOWN_SECONDS
	EN	Automatic recognition cooldown (seconds)

PLUGIN_SHAZAMCAPTURE_AUTO_COOLDOWN_SECONDS_DESC
	EN	Wait 30–900 seconds after a successful recognition or an exhausted trial sequence before starting the next automatic sample. Default: 120.

PLUGIN_SHAZAMCAPTURE_MANUAL_SAMPLE_MODE
	EN	Manual identification sample

PLUGIN_SHAZAMCAPTURE_MANUAL_SAMPLE_MODE_DESC
	EN	Buffered sample uses recent PCM and waits if needed to reach the configured initial length. Fresh sample clears PCM when identification is accepted, then waits for that same length. FFmpeg and PCM capture remain active in both modes.

PLUGIN_SHAZAMCAPTURE_MANUAL_SAMPLE_BUFFERED
	EN	Buffered sample

PLUGIN_SHAZAMCAPTURE_MANUAL_SAMPLE_FRESH
	EN	Fresh sample

PLUGIN_SHAZAMCAPTURE_SAMPLE_SECONDS
	EN	Initial sample length (seconds)

PLUGIN_SHAZAMCAPTURE_SAMPLE_SECONDS_DESC
	EN	Wait until 5–30 seconds of PCM are available, then analyze that initial sample. Default: 10.

PLUGIN_SHAZAMCAPTURE_RETRY_COUNT
	EN	No-match retries

PLUGIN_SHAZAMCAPTURE_RETRY_COUNT_DESC
	EN	Allow 0–10 additional Shazam attempts for no-matches or pending consecutive confirmation. Errors are not retried. Default: 1.

PLUGIN_SHAZAMCAPTURE_CONSECUTIVE_CONFIRMATIONS
	EN	Consecutive confirmations required

PLUGIN_SHAZAMCAPTURE_CONSECUTIVE_CONFIRMATIONS_DESC
	EN	Require the same Shazam result 1–11 times in a row before showing or saving it as a valid match. A value of 1 disables confirmation. Confirmation attempts use the existing retry count and delay. Default: 1.

PLUGIN_SHAZAMCAPTURE_SKIP_CONFIRMATIONS_AFTER_TWO_NO_MATCHES
	EN	Accept the first match after two no-results

PLUGIN_SHAZAMCAPTURE_SKIP_CONFIRMATIONS_AFTER_TWO_NO_MATCHES_DESC
	EN	If the first two Shazam attempts return no match, stop requiring consecutive confirmations for that recognition request and accept the next match. This requires at least two configured retries. Default: off.

PLUGIN_SHAZAMCAPTURE_RETRY_SAMPLE_SECONDS
	EN	Retry sample length (seconds)

PLUGIN_SHAZAMCAPTURE_RETRY_SAMPLE_SECONDS_DESC
	EN	Analyze the newest 5–30 seconds of buffered audio on each retry. Default: 10.

PLUGIN_SHAZAMCAPTURE_RETRY_DELAY_SECONDS
	EN	Delay between retries (seconds)

PLUGIN_SHAZAMCAPTURE_RETRY_DELAY_SECONDS_DESC
	EN	Wait 1–30 seconds before each retry so newer audio can enter the buffer. Default: 5.

PLUGIN_SHAZAMCAPTURE_PLAYER_SETTINGS_EMPTY
	EN	There are no configurable settings for this player yet.
